About BloodBridge

BloodBridge is a portfolio project demonstrating how a simple web platform can connect blood donors and people making blood requests.

Safety first

This demo does not verify donor eligibility, blood availability, hospital information, or emergency requests. For real emergencies, contact authorized emergency services or a registered blood bank/hospital.
